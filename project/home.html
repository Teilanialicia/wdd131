Welcome

This site is for those interested in visiting Java, Indonesia, it's temples, and culture. Java lies between Sumatra to the west and Bali to the east. It is a volcano-dotted island that is at the geographic and economic center of Indonesia, and home to more than half its people. Java's largest city is modern, sprawling Jakarta, the nation's capital.
